02 / THE PROBLEM

A voice shares its space with noise.

A nearby microphone hears speech and noise together. An ambient microphone hears a different view of the environment. The challenge is to reduce interference while preserving speech.

The optional example below is synthesized by the real processing engine. Its formant-like tone is not intelligible human speech, and it does not establish field performance.

04 / THREE NOISE BEHAVIORS

One policy cannot fit every interruption.

Explore the conditions the research engine models. These animated lines are educational illustrations, not measured traces.

STATIONARY

Continuous hum

Relatively steady interference gives the adaptive filter time to converge.

CHANGING

Moving conditions

Wind and crowd energy vary, requiring the filter to track change.

IMPULSIVE

Sudden transients

The prototype gates adaptation around abrupt events so coefficients do not chase a short spike.

06 / THE EVIDENCE

Hear it. Measure it. Keep the record.

Compare raw, NLMS, spectral, and hybrid outputs. SNR requires an aligned clean reference. STOI and PESQ only have meaning for appropriate real speech and are left unavailable when they cannot be justified.
